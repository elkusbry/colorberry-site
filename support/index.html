Colorberry Support

Colorberry is a coloring app for families on iPhone and iPad.

Contact us

Email bryan@4thcoach.com. We read every message.

Report a picture

If you see a picture that shouldn't be in Colorberry, email us at bryan@4thcoach.com and tell us what it is. Inside the app, a grown-up can also tap the flag on a friend's picture, or use Grown-ups > Help & privacy > Report. To stop seeing a friend's pictures right away, go to Grown-ups > Family & friends and tap Stop following.

Opening Grown-ups

On the home screen, press and hold the small Grown-ups button for 2 seconds, then type the three numbers that are spelled out.

Making new pictures

Making a new picture is a grown-ups-only feature. It uses Google Gemini with your own Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com. Add the key in Grown-ups > Picture key. You can set a daily limit there and see an estimate of the cost.

Family sharing

Kids' profiles and drawings can sync through your own iCloud. Turn it on in Grown-ups > Family & friends, where you can also invite the other parent and approve friends.

Deleting your data

Delete drawings or kids in the app, or delete the app. To remove iCloud copies, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage > Colorberry.
